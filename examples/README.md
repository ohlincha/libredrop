# Example recording

`water_on_glass.mkv`: about 1.8 µL of water placed on a glass slide (touch-off from the needle), Ossila
goniometer camera at 1920×1080, 30 fps, cropped to the band around the surface and compressed (grey, H.264).
16 s; the drop touches at 2.7 s and spreads from 70° to 20°. Scale 94.23 px/mm. Recorded 2026-11-24.
Built into the program (Help ▸ Open example).
