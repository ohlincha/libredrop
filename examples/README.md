# Example recording

`water_on_glass.mkv`: about 1.8 µL of water placed on a glass slide (touch-off from the needle), Ossila
goniometer camera at 1920×1080, 30 fps. Compact copy made by LibreDrop (`LibreDropCLI compact`, JPEG quality 60):
rows 464–799 (the band around the surface), grey, Motion-JPEG, so it opens without ffmpeg. 14 s, 418 frames; the
drop touches at 2.75 s and spreads from 70° to 20°. Scale 94.23 px/mm. Recorded 2026-11-24. Built into the
program (Help ▸ Open example).
